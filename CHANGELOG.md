# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- First version, for RimWorld 1.6.
- Two families of food no longer ask for a table: what is not a meal
  (`preferability < MealTerrible`) and preserved rations (meals keeping 20 days or more).
- Hunger excuses the meal. Past urgently hungry, or starving if preferred, the thought is
  skipped whatever the food was.
- Settings: one checkbox per family, one for hunger with its threshold, and the full list of
  affected foods with search and per-food override.
- English and French translations.
